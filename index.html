Why EDRA

Someone has to account for it.

Sustainability figures now sit beside financial ones, in annual reports, investor questionnaires and assurance scopes. But they rarely arrive like financial data. They come as utility bills, meter exports, vendor certificates and spreadsheets built for something else.

So gaps get closed with estimates. Blanks become zeros. A figure is restated and the earlier one quietly disappears. By the time someone asks where a number came from, nobody can say.

EDRA brings accounting-grade discipline to sustainability data. Not another form to fill: a ledger for the evidence behind every figure.

The platform

Three states. Never blurred.

Every figure on EDRA sits in exactly one state. A number never crosses from one to the next without a recorded step, so anyone can see what a figure is, and what it is not.

01

Submitted

What the company sent, exactly as sent. Workbooks and files are held unchanged and sorted without being rewritten.

A blank cell stays blank.
02

Governed

What an analyst accepted. Each accepted figure is bound to the file it came from, which is held and hashed.

An estimate is refused. A reply with no file waits.
03

Calculated

What a completed run derived. The platform records which inputs were governed and which were only submitted.

A rerun is new. The first is preserved.

Principles

The rules the platform will not break.

01

Missing is not zero.

An absent figure is an open ask. It is never written down as nil.

02

An estimate is not evidence.

A governed figure arrives with its source file, or it waits until it does.

03

A citation is not proof.

Naming a document is not the same as holding it. EDRA holds it.

04

Partial stays partial.

A sum is called a total only when every part is in, at every site.

05

Nothing is overwritten.

Each rerun is a new calculation. Every earlier one is preserved.

06

Not an assurance. Not a rating.

EDRA states what the evidence supports. Assurance stays with your assurer, and there is no score.
