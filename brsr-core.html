WHAT BRSR CORE ASKS FOR

Nine attributes. Each one read from your own records.

BRSR Core is SEBI's assurance-ready subset of BRSR. EDRA ONE maps every attribute to the documents that can support it, so you know from day one what you have, and what you still need.

01

Green-house gas footprint

Read from: fuel purchase records, electricity bills, published grid factors.

02

Water footprint

Read from: meter readings, tanker and municipal invoices, discharge records.

03

Energy footprint

Read from: electricity bills, fuel consumption, renewable energy certificates.

04

Embracing circularity

Read from: waste manifests, recycler and disposal receipts.

05

Employee wellbeing and safety

Read from: HR registers, wellbeing spend, safety incident logs.

06

Gender diversity in business

Read from: payroll and HR data, including wages paid to women.

07

Inclusive development

Read from: procurement from MSMEs and smaller towns, local hiring records.

08

Fairness with customers and suppliers

Read from: complaint registers, payables and supplier payment days.

09

Openness of business

Read from: purchase and sales concentration, related party transactions.
