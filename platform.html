Platform · EDRA ONE

One record. Every figure rooted in it.

EDRA ONE is our ESG data platform. It reads the records you keep, calculates only what they support, and keeps the reasoning behind every number. When someone asks how you arrived at a figure, the answer is already there.

See it on your own records Inside EDRA ONE
Three states, never blurred

You always know what a number is, and what it is not.

Every value in EDRA ONE sits in exactly one state. Nothing moves from one to the next without a recorded step, so a submitted figure is never shown as if it were checked.

01 · Submitted

What you sent

Workbooks and files, held exactly as received and fingerprinted on arrival. Sorting restructures them; it never fills a blank.

02 · Governed

What an analyst accepted

A figure enters the governed record through a recorded decision, bound to the file it came from and to the person who accepted it.

03 · Calculated

What a completed run derived

Each calculation is locked as it completed, and states which of its inputs were governed and which were only submitted.

Engagements

Your position, in three lines

Each engagement is one assessment, for one reporting entity, for one period. The latest completed calculation reads as answered, partial or not sayable, in plain words.

  • Send a workbook straight from the page
  • Answer open asks, each with its file
  • Only the latest calculation's own results are shown
Know

Every result, where it stands

The results of the latest calculation, one line per requirement: the value, its position, whether the data is measured, how many sites it covers, and whether it has been reviewed.

  • Coverage shown site by site, never rounded up
  • A missing input is named, not hidden
  • Open any result to see what it rests on
Act

What is open, and with whom

Every open ask in one list, grouped by who it is waiting on: you or the EDRA team. The counts always match the rows.

  • Needed from you, with the reason
  • What EDRA is handling, so you do not chase it
  • Calculation issues are ours to resolve
After your answers

A new calculation, beside the old

Once replies are accepted, EDRA ONE calculates again. The earlier run is kept exactly as it was, and the new position shows what your answers changed.

  • Full coverage is stated, not assumed
  • Not yet reviewed until a person reviews it
  • Any two calculations can be compared

From a board line back to the page it came from.

Security and control

Your records stay yours, and stay as sent.
