Approach

We work beside you, one season at a time.

EDRA is a platform and a team. You bring the records you already keep. We turn them into figures you can stand behind, chase what is missing, and stay for what comes after the filing.

Start the conversation See the cycle
Where it starts

Start with what you already have.

Most teams already hold the evidence. It sits in utility bills, meter exports, purchase invoices, HR registers and the workbook someone keeps by hand. We start there, in whatever shape it is in.

No template to fill first.

Send the files as they are. We map them; you do not reformat them for us.

Kept exactly as sent.

Every file is held unchanged and fingerprinted on arrival. A blank cell stays blank. Nothing is quietly corrected.

A first position, not a verdict.

You see what your records support today, and exactly what would complete the picture: a plain statement of where each figure stands.

The cycle

Know. Act. Record. Prove.

The same four steps run every reporting season. Each one has a clear owner, so nothing waits on a question nobody was asked.

01 · Know

Know what is true

EDRA ONE calculates each figure only where the evidence supports it, and names every requirement it cannot yet answer.

You see: your position, line by line

02 · Act

Act on what matters

Each gap becomes one specific ask: the figure, the site, the period, and the document it should come from. Your EDRA team does the chasing.

You see: a short list, owned by name

03 · Record

Record why

Every accepted figure keeps its source file, its factor and its method, with the decision that let it in.

You see: the reasoning behind every number

04 · Prove

Prove what changed

Each calculation is locked and kept. A new one never overwrites the last, so any two can be compared line by line.

You see: the difference, and what moved it

An engagement, season by season

From the first file to the next filing.

One entity, one reporting period, one governed record. This is how a season runs with us.

  1. Scope

    Agree what is being reported

    The entity, the period and the framework. Which sites are in, and who on your side holds which records.

  2. Send

    Share what you have

    One workbook and the files behind it. They are held exactly as sent; EDRA sorts them, it does not rewrite them.

  3. First calculation

    See your position

    A first run across every requirement in scope. Each line shows where it stands, and what would complete it.

  4. Close the gaps

    Answer the asks

    You send the missing figure with the file it came from. An analyst accepts or rejects each reply, and the decision is recorded.

    Every reply travels with its source. Estimates wait for your sign-off.
  5. Rerun

    Calculate again

    Accepted figures go into a fresh calculation, kept beside the one before it.

    The first calculation is preserved.
  6. File

    File with confidence

    A locked, traceable record, ready for your assurance or assessment provider to examine document by document.

  7. Between filings

    Make the next one better

    We help you choose where to improve, then measure whether it worked when the next season's records arrive.
