About EDRA

The bridge to sustainability data.

Sustainability data is where the world is moving, and someone has to account for it. EDRA brings accounting-grade discipline to that data, so it can be measured, trusted and reported with the same confidence as the financials beside it.

What EDRA is

A ledger for the evidence behind every figure.

EDRA is the data layer for sustainability reporting. It takes the workbooks, bills, meter exports and certificates a company already holds, calculates only what they support, and turns every gap into a specific request rather than an estimate.

Every accepted figure stays bound to the file it came from. Every calculation is kept, so a number can always be traced back to the run, the decision and the document behind it.

One governed core answers many frameworks. BRSR Core is live for Indian listed companies today; GRESB, IGBC and NABERS are launching soon.
