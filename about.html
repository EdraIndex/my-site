About EDRA

The bridge to sustainability data.

Sustainability data is where the world is moving, and someone has to account for it. EDRA brings accounting-grade discipline to that data, so it can be measured, trusted and reported with the same confidence as the financials beside it.

What EDRA is

A platform and a team, built around the evidence.

EDRA ONE, our ESG data platform, turns the records a company already keeps into figures every framework can trust. It calculates only what the evidence supports, turns each gap into a specific request, and keeps the reasoning behind every number.

Beside it sits the EDRA team: the people who find the missing month, explain the factor, and help you decide what to change next. BRSR Core is live in India today; GRESB, IGBC and NABERS are coming soon.
