BRSR Core

The assured core of Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting.

SEBI requires listed companies in India to report on their environmental, social and governance performance through BRSR. BRSR Core is the assured subset: nine attributes that must stand up to examination.

Who reports
Listed companies in India, under SEBI's disclosure requirements
What it asks
Nine BRSR Core attributes, from greenhouse gas footprint to openness of business

Live. Each attribute is mapped to the documents that can support it, and calculated from your own records

GRESB

The ESG benchmark for real estate and infrastructure investors.

GRESB assesses real estate and infrastructure portfolios every year, asset by asset. It asks for performance data on energy, greenhouse gas emissions, water and waste, and rewards coverage that can be evidenced.

Who reports
Real estate and infrastructure funds, owners and developers, for their investors
What it asks
Asset-level performance data and the management practices behind it, each year
On EDRA ONE
The same asset-level evidence a BRSR figure rests on, read for GRESB. Our team has worked GRESB cycles for over a decade

IGBC

Indian Green Building Council ratings for buildings and campuses.

IGBC rating systems recognise buildings and campuses for how they are designed and how they perform, including the energy and water they use.

Who uses it
Developers, owners and occupiers of buildings and campuses in India
What it asks
Evidence of design and operational performance, including energy and water

NABERS

National ratings for the measured performance of buildings.

NABERS rates buildings on how they actually perform, from twelve months of measured data such as energy and water use. It rewards real operation, not design intent.

Who uses it
Owners, managers and tenants of offices, shopping centres, hotels and other buildings
What it asks
Twelve months of measured utility data for the rated building
